# Ajudante de Lição

A homework helper game for kids (Portuguese UI) that makes math and spelling practice fun. Progress is saved in the browser.

Run: open `index.html` in a browser, or `python3 -m http.server 5179` in this folder and open http://localhost:5179

- **Corrida das Contas**: a race powered by quick answers to multiplication tables, addition, subtraction and division
- **Soletrando**: listen to a word and spell it, in Portuguese or English
- **Treinar meus erros**: practice the problems and words you got wrong
- **Álbum de Figurinhas**: collect stickers by doing well

Structure: `index.html` (screens), `game.js` (game logic), `style.css`.
